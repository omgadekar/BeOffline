# BeOffline at a Glance

BeOffline helps you make selected apps truly offline, on your terms. Instead of only muting notifications or hiding alerts, it blocks internet access for the apps you choose so they stop pulling in new messages, updates, and distractions while the rest of your phone stays usable. The result is a stronger kind of focus: you decide when you are reachable, when you are unavailable, and when certain apps simply do not get access to your attention.

# What Problem BeOffline Solves

Most focus tools soften interruptions without actually stopping them. You can silence notifications, turn on Do Not Disturb, or hide badges, but the messages are still arriving in the background. The moment you open the app, everything catches up at once and the distraction comes flooding back in.

BeOffline is designed for people who want more intentional boundaries. It is for the moments when you do not just want less noise, you want certain apps to stop being connected for a while. That creates a cleaner mental break for deep work, study, rest, family time, or simply protecting your mood from conversations you are not ready to deal with yet.

# What BeOffline Does

BeOffline lets you create rules that block internet access for selected apps. Each rule can contain one app or a group of apps, and each rule can be turned on or off from the dashboard.

The app currently supports three ways to control access:

- Permanent rules that stay on until you turn them off.
- Scheduled rules that run during chosen days and times.
- Timer rules that stay active for a chosen duration.

This makes BeOffline flexible enough for instant boundaries, repeating routines, and focused sessions that start and end automatically.

# How BeOffline Works

BeOffline uses Android's VPN framework locally on your device to control which apps stay online and which ones go offline. When you activate a rule, the selected apps are routed into BeOffline's local control layer and their internet access is blocked, while the rest of your phone can continue working as normal.

In simple terms, it does not just quiet the app down. It stops that app from reaching the internet while the rule is active. That is why the experience feels different from ordinary notification silencing.

Like any Android app that depends on background behavior, real-world reliability can vary somewhat by device manufacturer and battery settings. BeOffline is built to be resilient, but Android behavior is not identical on every phone.

# Frequently Asked Questions

- **Is this just notification blocking?**  
  No. BeOffline is designed to block internet access for selected apps while a rule is active, which is a stronger boundary than simply muting alerts.

- **Can I block multiple apps together?**  
  Yes. A single rule can include one app or a group of apps.

- **Can I use schedules and timers?**  
  Yes. BeOffline supports permanent rules, recurring schedules, and timer-based sessions.

- **Can I still open an app while it is offline?**  
  You can still open the app, but while the rule is active its internet access is restricted by BeOffline.

- **Will it work on all Android phones the same way?**  
  Not always. Android manufacturers handle background activity differently, so behavior can vary somewhat by device and settings.

- **What if I run into a bug?**  
  You can submit a report directly from inside the app using the built-in issue reporting flow.
